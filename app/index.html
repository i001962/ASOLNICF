Generalized Metalog Table — inscribe the lookup

2,204 cells (19 σ rows × 116 N columns). Each cell holds the 9 metalog a-coefficients for the sum of IID lognormals (Keelin/Chrisman/Savage 2019). Minting a cell writes its values on chain, verified by merkle proof — no admin, no trust. Proceeds go to the Base revnet (project 13) with you as beneficiary. Minters are permanently recorded: once complete, any contract can call getCoeffs().
